Designed and evaluated a scalable online support ecosystem for the OpenSTEM robotics program, focused on enabling global educators to successfully adopt and teach robotics using low-cost XRP robots. The project aimed to create a self-sustaining learning community that combined course delivery, technical support, discussion forums, and community engagement into an accessible platform for international participants.

## Key Contributions

Defined platform requirements for course delivery, technical Q&A, community building, moderation, accessibility, and long-term sustainability.
Conducted a trade study comparing platforms including Discord, Slack, Teams, WhatsApp, Canvas, Reddit, and Stack Overflow using criteria such as affordability, accessibility, maintenance effort, searchability, privacy, and response time.
Developed a prototype community structure with dedicated channels, discussion forums, and moderation workflows to support both technical problem solving and community engagement.
Designed an Agile testing and feedback process to iteratively improve the platform through pilot deployments and user feedback cycles.
Analyzed challenges related to technology adoption, onboarding, and long-term sustainability for global STEM education initiatives.

## Methods & Tools

* Trade Studies and Decision Matrices
* User Experience and Community Design
* Agile Development and Iterative Testing
* Educational Technology Evaluation
* Stakeholder and Requirements Analysis

Results

The evaluation identified Discord as the strongest overall platform for community engagement and technical support, leading to the development of a prototype support community for the OpenSTEM XRP robotics program. The project established a framework for scalable educator support, peer-to-peer learning, and sustainable community growth across international deployments.
