Hello, I'm Tom,

I am a software engineer currently working on (MathCAT)[https://github.com/daisy/MathCAT], an open-source assistive technology library for converting MathML to speech, braille, and math navigation.

Previously I worked at Neurala as a research scientist connecting the research and engineering sides of the company. I participated in the research of new suitable models for the product, while also developing an automated evaluation system for new models.

I graduated from Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) with a bachelor's and master's in robotics, as well as a minor in CS. During my master's, I did my thesis with Professor Leahy at the (Automata Lab)[https://wp.wpi.edu/automata/]. I also worked for three years as a student assistant/teaching assistant, teaching the core robotics courses for the undergraduate robotics curriculum, and I have served as both the president and student representative of the robotics honor society at WPI.
