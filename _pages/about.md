About me
======

Hello, I'm Tom,

I am a robotics software engineer currently working at MathCAT to develop software for an open source library 

Previously I worked at Neurala as a research scientist whose main job was connecting the research and engineering sides of the company. I participated in the research of new suitable models for the product, while also developing an automated evaluation system for new models. 

I graduated from Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) with a bachelors and masters in robotics, as well as a minor in CS. During my masters I did my thesis with Professor Leahy at the Automata Lab (https://wp.wpi.edu/automata/). I also worked for three years as a student assistant/teaching assistant, teaching the main core robotics courses for the undergraduate robotics curriculum. I also got to be both the president and student representative for the robotics honor society at WPI.
